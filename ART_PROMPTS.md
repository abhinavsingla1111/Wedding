# Artwork prompts

Use the same chat for all images so the style stays consistent. Shared style line, put it at the end of every prompt:

> Style: soft romantic painted illustration, delicate watercolour and gouache, luminous and dreamy, fine detail, warm ivory, cream, blush pink, sage green and antique gold palette with a few deep wine-red roses. No text, no letters, no watermark, no border.

## 1. hero.jpg — opening scene (portrait 9:16)

> A vertical 9:16 painted wedding invitation background. A grand carved ivory-marble Mughal arch fills the frame: intricate jali lattice and floral relief carving in the spandrels, slender columns with ornate capitals on both sides. Delicate climbing vines with tiny white blossoms trail down over the arch. Through the arch, a misty garden lake at soft golden morning light, with willow trees and a hazy palace silhouette far in the distance. In the lower third, two graceful white mute swans face each other on the calm water, their necks curving to form a heart, with soft mirror reflections and gentle ripples. The upper and middle part of the arch opening is an empty, softly lit cream sky with nothing in it, leaving clean space for text in the centre.

## 2. hero.mp4 — optional animation (Sora, Veo, Kling or Runway: image-to-video from hero.jpg)

> Camera completely still. The two swans glide very slowly towards each other and back, the water ripples softly with shimmering light, the vines sway gently in a light breeze, a few tiny petals drift down. Calm, slow, seamless 8–10 second loop.

## 3. flowers-corner.png — bouquet (square, transparent background)

> A lush painted flower cluster designed for the bottom-left corner of an invitation: garden roses and peonies in blush pink and ivory with two or three deep wine-red roses, sage-green leaves, small white blossoms and trailing vines. The flowers sit in the bottom-left corner and fan outwards up and to the right. Isolated on a fully transparent background, no shadow.

## 4. gurudwara.png — Anand Karaj (landscape 4:3)

> A serene white-marble Sikh Gurudwara Sahib with a large golden onion dome, smaller golden domes and chhatris, arched doorways, and a saffron Nishan Sahib flag on a tall pole, glowing in soft golden morning light, a calm sarovar in front with a gentle reflection, a few flowering trees at the sides. The edges fade softly into a plain warm cream background.

## 5. couple.png — finale (portrait 4:5)

> A soft painted illustration with a gentle anime feel of an Indian Punjabi wedding couple, full body, standing side by side and smiling. The groom is a Sikh Sardar with a neatly tied blush-pink pagg (turban) with a gold kalgi, a full dark beard, an ivory sherwani with gold buttons and a rose-pink palla stole over his shoulder. The bride wears a deep wine-red bridal lehenga with gold embroidery, a dupatta draped over her head, maang tikka, nath and red chooda, and she holds the end of the groom's palla. Plain soft cream background fading out at the edges.
